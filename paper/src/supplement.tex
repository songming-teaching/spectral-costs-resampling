\section{Solver Unit Tests}\label{sec:S23}

All spectral predictions in the main text are computed from the fixed-point
equation of Definition~\ref{main-def:Tc} by two independent implementations (covariance side
and companion side, the latter via the auxiliary variable $u=c\,m$ and
$e=u+(c-1)/z$). The battery below is run before any experiment; each row
reports the realized tolerance.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
Check & Quantity & Realized & Criterion \\
\midrule
Branch & $\operatorname{Im}m(z)$ at $z=\mathrm{i},\,3+2\mathrm{i},\,0.05+0.5\mathrm{i}$
  & $0.610,\ 0.263,\ 0.919$ & $>0$ \\
Equation residual & $\max$ over grid & $2.2\times10^{-13}$ & $\ll 10^{-8}$ \\
Normalization & $z\,m(z)$ at $z=10^{6}\mathrm{i}$ & $-1.000000+10^{-6}\mathrm{i}$ & $\to-1$ \\
$\mathrm{MP}(c)$ recovery & max interior relative density error & $0.760\%$ & $<1\%$ \\
Zero-mass consistency & max $|$solver $-$ analytic$|$, three configurations
  & $4.3\times10^{-8}$ & $\ll10^{-6}$ \\
Truncation $K{=}20$ vs $K{=}60$ & $\max|\Delta f|$ on grid & $2.1\times10^{-15}$ & $\ll10^{-8}$ \\
 & discarded second tail moment & $5.1\times10^{-15}$ & $\ll10^{-8}$ \\
Dual-solver cross-check & transform-relation residual & $6.4\times10^{-13}$ & $\ll10^{-8}$ \\
 & companion identity $\int(1+tu)^{-1}=-z\,e$ residual & $1.9\times10^{-13}$ & $\ll10^{-8}$ \\
 & density deviation, $x>0.15$ & $0.000\%$ & $<2\%$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The bandwidth sensitivity $\max|\Delta f|=0.1438$ at $x=0.020$ between
$h=10^{-3}$ and $h=5\times10^{-4}$ is edge sharpening at the left boundary
layer and is reported rather than tested; all conclusions in the main text are
drawn away from this layer, and the $h/2$ sensitivity check reported there
leaves every conclusion unchanged. The classical companion fixed-point forms
found in the literature govern the companion transform of the $W$-side model
and do not apply to the object studied here; the cross-check above is the
reason the two implementations cannot silently agree on a wrong equation.

\textbf{Separable population spectrum (A3).}
$H=\tfrac12\delta_{1/2}+\tfrac12\delta_{3/2}$, uniform weights, multinomial
resampling, $c=0.8$: post-resampling $m_1=1.0011$ (theory $a_1=1$); $m_2$ pre

control, the $H\boxtimes\mathcal T_c(\operatorname{Poi}(1))$ pipeline
(S-transform computation) reproduces a directly simulated separable Wishart
spectrum with maximum Stieltjes deviation $5.1\times10^{-4}$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:a3}).
